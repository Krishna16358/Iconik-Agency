How We Work

No Confusion. No Surprises.

A clear, step-by-step process that takes your business from first conversation to a live, growing digital presence.

01
Week 1

Discovery

We start with a focused conversation about your business, your customers, your goals, and what's currently working or not working online. This call sets the foundation for everything that follows.

Free 30-minute consultation call
Audit of your current online presence
Competitor and market research
Clear project scope and deliverables
02
Week 1–2

Strategy

Before a single pixel is designed, we build a clear strategy. This covers your positioning, the structure of your site, the message, and how every element will work together to attract and convert customers.

Brand positioning and messaging
Website structure and page plan
Content and copy direction
Timeline and milestone sign-off
03
Week 2–4

Build

Design and development happens fast. We share progress at every checkpoint and take your feedback seriously. You'll never be left wondering where your project is — we communicate proactively throughout.

Custom design based on your brand
Mobile-first responsive build
2 rounds of feedback included
Testing across all devices
SEO setup and speed optimization
04
Ongoing

Growth

Launch is the beginning, not the end. We monitor your site's performance, track what's working, and keep improving. Whether you want ongoing support or just a maintenance package — we're here for the long run.

Go-live checklist and launch support
Analytics and performance tracking
Monthly maintenance and updates
Ongoing growth strategy calls
Priority support via WhatsApp
